About SupplStore

About SupplStore | Independent supplement buyer guide

SupplStore is an independent editorial buyer-guide platform for dietary supplements sold in the United States. We do not manufacture, stock, ship, or process orders for any product covered on this site. We research, review, verify, and compare what is actually on the market — then route buyers to the official manufacturer source for each product.

What we do

  • Research and review dietary supplements across categories: men's wellness, weight management, cognitive support, blood sugar support, joint & nerve support, sensory & sleep wellness, digestive wellness, and general wellness
  • Verify each product's single official manufacturer source so buyers do not end up on counterfeit third-party listings
  • Track ingredient disclosure, manufacturer credentials (FDA-registered facility, GMP certification), and refund-window honesty
  • Synthesize buyer-experience patterns from publicly available signals to help readers form realistic expectations

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, ship, fulfill, or process orders
  • We do not stock or warehouse inventory
  • We do not handle returns, refunds, or customer service for any product
  • We are not affiliated with, owned by, or operated by any single supplement manufacturer

For any order, shipping, refund, or product-support question, please contact the official manufacturer of the product directly — they are the only party who can fulfill those requests.

How we earn

We may earn a commission when readers purchase through affiliate links on this site — at no additional cost to the reader. Commissions do not influence our editorial ratings or methodology. Affiliate links are clearly marked with rel="sponsored" per FTC 16 CFR Part 255 guidance on endorsements and material connections.

Editorial methodology

Every product we publish a review for passes a four-screen audit:

  1. Official source verification — we confirm a single canonical manufacturer page and reject products without one
  2. Ingredient and dose transparency — products with hidden formulas or proprietary-blend-only labeling are screened out
  3. U.S. GMP-facility confirmation — manufacturer must operate in a registered, GMP-compliant facility
  4. Refund-window honesty — money-back guarantee terms must be clearly stated and enforceable through the manufacturer

Products that fail any of these four screens are not published.

Editorial team and medical disclaimer

Reviews are produced by the SupplStore editorial team. Our coverage is for informational and educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We are not licensed medical professionals. Readers should consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any supplement regimen, especially when pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or managing a medical condition.

Contact

For editorial corrections, fact-check requests, or product coverage suggestions, see the Contact page. For any order, refund, or shipping question please contact the official manufacturer of the product — those requests cannot be processed by this website.
